Method: Mouser product pages were read directly (unit price at the 1-pc and 10-pc breaks, stock, lifecycle flag). Digi-Key blocks automated page access (Cloudflare), so Digi-Key figures below come from Digi-Key search-result snippets only; treat them as qty-1 indications, not a verified price table. Where neither Digi-Key nor Mouser could be read, LCSC is quoted and labelled as such. Confidence key: HIGH = exact MPN identified from the design's own device/footprint name. MEDIUM = series/footprint identified from the design, specific value, voltage or tolerance chosen here. LOW/TBD = guess or no part.

## Notes

1. **CIN is not a Kemet 1210 part.** KTS500B106M55N0T00 is Nippon Chemi-Con NTS-series (the Allegro footprint literally says CAP_NTS_55_2P8T = 5.7 x 5.0 mm body, 2.8 mm thick, i.e. EIA 2220). Chemi-Con lists it as In Production, but distributor stock is thin (Mouser 126 pcs, LCSC 0). If it goes short, in-production 2220 50 V 10 uF alternatives to price: KEMET C2220C106K5RACTU (X7R), Murata GRM55DR61H106KA88L (X5R). Any 1210 substitute would need a footprint change.
2. **C23 value conflict.** The device name embeds GJM1555C1H220JB01 (22 pF) while the schematic value reads 47 pF. If this is a crystal load cap, 22 pF is the more typical value for an 8 MHz HSE crystal with ~18 pF CL, but the schematic wins until someone checks the crystal spec. Both parts priced above.
3. **L1 SLF7045T-4R7M2R0-PF is obsolete** (TDK PCN 2024-07-30; Mouser lifecycle = Obsolete; Digi-Key still shows residual stock). Mouser's listed recommended alternative is TDK VLS6045EX-4R7M-H (6.0 x 6.0 x 4.5 mm, 4.7 uH, 4.2 A). The TDK PCN itself names VLS5045EX-4R7M (5 x 5 mm). Either requires a new footprint; the 6 x 6 part is the closer mechanical fit to the 7 x 7 original. Neither substitute could be priced at Digi-Key/Mouser today (LCSC $0.21 for VLS6045EX-4R7M-H).
4. **U5 TCAN1042HDRQ1 lifecycle.** TI's own part-details page reports ACTIVE (TI store out of stock), while Mouser tags it "End of Life: scheduled for obsolescence". TI's product page points to TCAN1044A-Q1 as a newer, pin-for-pin, same-function device (SOIC-8: TCAN1044ADRQ1). Buy TCAN1042HDRQ1 from current distributor stock for V1, but expect to move to TCAN1044A-Q1. Non-automotive TCAN1042HDR is the same silicon without AEC-Q100 if that is acceptable.
5. **R5 ROHM MCR10EZPF3002** could not be found at Mouser, and Digi-Key carries a ROHM notice that MCR/MNR-series new orders are no longer accepted. Substitute Yageo RC0805FR-0730KL (or Panasonic ERJ-6ENF3002V) - same 0805 footprint, 30 k 1% 1/8 W.
6. **R6 Samsung RC1005F103CS** is Digi-Key-only among the two named distributors; qty-1 $0.10 is from a Digi-Key search snippet. Yageo RC0402FR-0710KL is a drop-in if you consolidate on one resistor vendor.
7. **Sullins PPTC headers and JST B4B-XH-A(LF)(SN)**: Mouser does not stock Sullins and did not render pricing for the JST header; the Digi-Key qty-1 numbers come from search snippets (multiple values were seen, the lowest is quoted in the table). Verify the qty-10 break on Digi-Key before ordering.
8. **Test points.** The 1.4 mm / 3.4 mm amv_test_point pad does not match any Keystone through-hole test point hole size (0.040 in miniature/loop, 0.063-0.078 in large). Recommend leaving these as bare PTH pads (no BOM line) for V1; if a loop is wanted, swap the footprint to a Keystone 5020/5021 two-hole 0.040 in pattern.
9. **CL10A 4.7 uF voltage.** The design encodes only series + size. 10 V (KP8) is the safer choice for a 3.3 V/5 V rail and is cheaper than the 6.3 V (KQ8) part today; a 16 V CL10A475KO8NNNC also exists if CEXT sits on a higher rail.
10. **Kyocera AVX global part numbers.** Mouser cross-lists 06035A471JAT2A to new global PN KGM15ACG1H471JT; both are orderable, same part.
11. **Murata 10 uF caps.** GRM21BR61C106KE15L and GRM31CR71E106KA12L both priced, but Mouser showed no in-stock quantity on the page read - check stock at order time. 50 V 1206 X5R (GRM31CR61H106KA12L) is obsolete at Mouser, hence the 25 V X7R choice for COUT (buck output, Vout <= 15 V).
12. **U6 output current.** Distributor listings show R1240N001B at ~1.2 A. If V1 intends to feed servo/ESC logic from this rail, confirm the load budget.
13. **Mouser tariff banner.** Several Mouser passive pages showed "a tariff of X% may be applied if shipping to the United States" (8% to 30%). Not included in the figures.
