What must exist in circuitry

Independent island-power authority, verified reference validity, main 3V3 and local 5V observation, receiver readiness, watchdog and stop continuity. After valid clear and a fresh healthy interval: release qualification, wait the recovery guard, accept a new ACK with ARM low, then accept a later ARM edge.

U14 still uses main 3V3. A hold-up capacitor on the observer does not make U14 valid during collapse. An exact output-isolation boundary must prevent invalid drive and remain disabled after stored energy expires.
