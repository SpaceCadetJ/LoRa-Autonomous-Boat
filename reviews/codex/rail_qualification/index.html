What these intervals mean

The primary calculation follows the rail-referenced accuracy reading in TPS3702 section 6.3.3 and encompasses the rounded nominal table. Table 7-1's example motivates a separate trip-relative sensitivity in the data file. These are conditional engineering screens, not guaranteed silicon bounds.

Recovery uses hysteresis independently from first fault assertion. Converter inputs exclude complete temperature, aging, ripple and load-transient bounds; the 5 V PFM upper limit is unknown. The 3.0–3.6 V corridor retains ACT-04's logic timing conditions. The proposed 4.5–5.5 V comparison is neither the full TXU operating range nor a measured receiver limit.
