How to interpret this study

Input current = [3.3 × logic current ÷ logic efficiency + 5 × (servo + other 5 V current) ÷ 5 V efficiency] ÷ input voltage

  • At 7 V, the 1.2 A logic and 2 A servo goals need 1.994 A even at 100% efficiency and zero auxiliary load. They cannot share a 1 A feed throughout the stated range.
  • The proposed 2 A, 5 V converter must supply both servo and auxiliary loads. A 2 A servo load alone leaves no allowance for the control circuitry.
  • Excluded: front-end and wiring losses, quiescent current, inrush, servo stall pulses, faults, the ESC motor and external-BEC power. A result within a planning limit does not qualify the fuse, connectors, copper, thermal behavior or hardware.
  • The 6S requirement and optional on-board actuator supply remain. Initial planning uses external BEC while the higher-load variant remains held.
